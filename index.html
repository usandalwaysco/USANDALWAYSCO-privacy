Privacy Policy — USANDALWAYSCO / Etsy Growth AI

Last Updated: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Etsy Growth AI, an internal shop-management and growth tool built and operated for the Etsy shop USANDALWAYSCO, handles data. Etsy Growth AI is used solely by the shop owner/operator to analyze their own Etsy shop and, where the owner explicitly connects a social media account, to prepare (but not automatically publish) content for that account.

Etsy Growth AI is not a public consumer application. It does not have a general public user base; it is operated by the shop owner for their own business.

1. Who We Are

Etsy Growth AI is operated by the individual/business behind the Etsy shop USANDALWAYSCO. For any privacy-related inquiry, contact:

usandalwayse@gmail.com

2. What Data We Access

From Etsy (read-only):

  • Shop information (shop name, shop ID)
  • Active listing data (title, description, tags, price, state, view/favorite counts)
  • Order/receipt data limited to non-personal fields: order status, payment/shipping/tax totals, currency, country, and an anonymous buyer account identifier

We do not store buyer names, shipping addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, gift messages, or personalization text entered by buyers. These fields are deliberately excluded from storage, even though Etsy's API returns them.

From connected social platforms (e.g., Pinterest, TikTok, Instagram, X), only if and when the shop owner explicitly connects an account:

  • Basic account identity: account ID and account/display name
  • Granted permission scopes and token expiry (technical metadata only)

We request only the minimum permission scopes needed to verify account identity. We do not request or use permissions to post, publish, or upload content on your behalf at this time.

3. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not automatically publish, post, or upload any content to any social platform.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.
  • We do not store social media access tokens or Etsy API tokens in any database; they are kept only in a local, non-public environment configuration file on the operator's own machine.
  • We do not use collected data to build advertising profiles.

4. How Data Is Stored

Etsy shop data (listings, orders, analytics) is stored in a local SQLite database on the shop operator's own computer. It is not hosted on a public server and is not accessible to anyone other than the shop operator.

5. How Data Is Used

  • To analyze the shop's own listing and order performance (internal analytics only).
  • To generate SEO and content suggestions for the shop owner's own review.
  • To verify the identity of a connected social media account before any future content-scheduling feature is used.
  • To prepare draft video content, using only the shop's own real, current listing data (price, title, favorite/view counts as provided by Etsy), for the shop owner's manual review.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Data is retained locally for as long as the shop operator continues to use the tool. The shop operator may delete the local database or revoke any connected social account's access at any time through that platform's own account settings. To request information about data handling, contact usandalwayse@gmail.com.

7. Third-Party Services

Etsy Growth AI connects to the official Etsy Open API and, where the operator chooses to connect them, the official APIs of social platforms such as Pinterest, TikTok, Instagram, and X. Use of those platforms is also subject to their own respective privacy policies and terms of service. Etsy Growth AI is an independent tool and is not endorsed by, affiliated with, or sponsored by Etsy, Pinterest, TikTok, Meta/Instagram, or X.

8. Children's Privacy

Etsy Growth AI is a business operations tool and is not directed at or intended for use by children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the tool's functionality changes. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.

10. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: usandalwayse@gmail.com
